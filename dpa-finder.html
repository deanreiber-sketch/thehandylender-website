How these numbers are calculated

FHA upfront mortgage insurance (MIP)1.75% of the base loan amount, financed into your first mortgage — not paid at closing.
FHA monthly mortgage insurance0.55% of the loan balance per year, divided into 12 monthly payments added to your mortgage payment.
Down payment covered by assistanceAll four programs cover your 3.5% FHA down payment, so you need little to no cash for the down payment.
Arrive Home ratesRepayable: 6.25% first / 8.25% second (10-yr amortizing). Forgivable: 6.625% first / 0% second (forgiven after 36 on-time payments).
metroDPA silent second0% interest, deferred — no monthly payment, but the full DPA balance is due when you sell or refinance. Never forgiven.
