About Dean

Good families deserve better guidance.

Most lenders disappear after closing. Dean Reiber built The Handy Lender™ because he kept watching good families navigate some of the biggest financial decisions of their lives without enough support — and that bothered him.

These tools exist because homeownership isn't a transaction. It's a relationship that spans decades. The right guidance changes what's possible.

  • Honest about what makes sense for your situation — including when the answer is "not yet"
  • Free tools built around homeowner anxiety, not mortgage categories
  • Guidance that continues long after you close — because that's when the real questions start
  • Colorado-specific knowledge that generic lenders simply don't have
