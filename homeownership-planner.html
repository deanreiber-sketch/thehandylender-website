How these numbers are calculated

FHA upfront MIP (1.75%)Financed into your first mortgage — not paid at closing, so it doesn't affect cash needed upfront.
FHA monthly mortgage insurance0.55% of the loan balance per year, split across 12 monthly payments.
Down payment covered by DPAAll four programs cover your 3.5% FHA down payment. Closing costs vary and may require additional funds.
Arrive Home ratesRepayable: 6.25% first / 8.25% second (10-yr amortizing). Forgivable: 6.625% first / 0% (forgiven after 36 on-time payments).
metroDPA silent second0% interest, deferred — no monthly payment. The full DPA balance is due when you sell or refinance. Never forgiven.
Cash needed estimateReflects estimated closing costs (~2–3% of purchase price) minus DPA. Your actual figure will vary — get a Loan Estimate for precision.

Important: This tool is for educational purposes only. It does not constitute a loan commitment, rate lock, or guarantee of financing. All rates, program terms, and assistance amounts are subject to change. Actual payments vary based on credit profile, property taxes, insurance, and other factors. FHA MIP reflects current standard rates. Arrive Home Forgivable forgiveness requires 36 consecutive on-time payments and other program conditions. metroDPA balance is due upon sale, refinance, or when the property is no longer your primary residence.
