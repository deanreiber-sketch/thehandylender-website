Exterior & Roof4 tasks
Inspect roof for missing shingles, loose flashing & ice dam damage
Look for lifted shingles, exposed nail heads, soft spots, and damaged flashing around chimneys and vents
Catching one problem early: $200–$500. Left until fall: $5,000–$15,000 in water damage
High
Clean gutters & downspouts thoroughly
Clear winter debris, check for sagging, and confirm downspouts direct water at least 6 feet from the foundation
Cleaning costs $100–$200. Blocked gutters are a leading cause of $3,000–$8,000 foundation and fascia repairs
High
Check exterior caulking around windows, doors & trim
Replace cracked or separated caulking — pay special attention to window corners and door thresholds
A $12 tube of caulk now. A $2,000–$4,000 mold remediation claim later if moisture gets behind siding
Med
Inspect siding & paint for cracks, rot or peeling
Touch up peeling paint and seal any cracks before Colorado's spring moisture works its way in
$200 paint touch-up today prevents $3,000+ siding replacement when moisture gets underneath
Med
Landscaping & Drainage3 tasks
Check grading — water must drain away from foundation
Soil should slope away from your home at least 6 inches over 10 feet. Snowmelt is the biggest spring test
Regrading costs $500–$2,000. Foundation repairs from poor drainage average $5,000–$15,000 in Colorado
High
Inspect sprinkler system after winter shutdown
Check for cracked heads, broken lines, and overspray hitting the foundation or siding
A $75 service call catches issues. Overspray against siding over a season leads to $1,500+ in rot repairs
Med
Trim trees & shrubs away from roof, siding & power lines
Colorado wind storms are fast and powerful — overhanging branches become projectiles
Trimming costs $150–$600. A fallen branch through a roof can cost $3,000–$10,000 in storm damage
Med
HVAC & Mechanical3 tasks
Replace HVAC filter
Colorado's dry spring air carries dust and allergens — a fresh filter protects both air quality and system efficiency
A $20 filter every 1–3 months. Neglecting filters shortens HVAC lifespan — system replacement runs $5,000–$12,000
High
Schedule AC service before summer heat arrives
Refrigerant, coils, electrical connections, and drain line — book early before Colorado's summer rush
Annual service: $100–$175. Emergency AC call during a July heat wave: $300–$600. System failure: $4,000–$10,000
High
Check exterior hose bibs for freeze damage or leaks
Turn on and let run — watch for drips, reduced pressure, or water appearing inside the wall
A cracked hose bib costs $75–$150 to replace. Undetected, it can cause thousands in wall and flooring damage

Cooling & Energy4 tasks
Seal air leaks around doors, windows & utility penetrations
Use caulk for stationary gaps, weatherstripping for moving parts — even small gaps add up fast at altitude
Air leaks account for 25–40% of heating and cooling loss. Sealing them can save $200–$600 annually in Colorado's climate
High
Inspect attic ventilation
Blocked soffit or ridge vents trap summer heat, baking the underside of your roof decking and shingles
Poor attic ventilation degrades shingles from above, cutting a $15,000 roof's lifespan by 5–7 years
High
Reverse ceiling fans to counterclockwise for summer
Counterclockwise creates a cooling downdraft — check the small switch on the motor housing
Proper fan direction reduces perceived temperature by 4°F, meaningfully reducing AC usage and energy costs
Med
Inspect & repair window screens
Torn screens invite insects and reduce natural ventilation efficiency — quick fix, big comfort improvement
Screen repair: $10–$30 per window. Neglected screens let insects in year-round and reduce natural ventilation
Med
Exterior Maintenance3 tasks
Inspect decks & fences for rot, loose boards & rusted fasteners
Probe wood with a screwdriver — if it sinks in, you have rot. Check all ledger board connections carefully
Annual inspection and staining extends deck life by decades. Full replacement runs $8,000–$25,000
High
Pressure wash siding, concrete, decks & patios
Removes mold, mildew, and UV-degraded surface — Colorado's sun and altitude accelerate surface breakdown
$150–$300 to wash. Mold remediation on siding or decking averages $1,500–$4,000 once it penetrates
Med
Seal concrete cracks in driveways, walks & patios
Fill any crack wider than a quarter — Colorado's freeze-thaw cycles expand cracks aggressively each winter
A $20 crack sealant now. Left through another Colorado winter, cracks expand into $500–$2,000 in concrete replacement
Med
Safety & Storm Prep3 tasks
Test smoke & CO detectors, replace batteries
Test monthly, replace batteries annually — CO is odorless and can be lethal, especially at Colorado's altitude
Working detectors cut home fire death risk by 50%. There is no cost-benefit calculation on this one
High
Test sump pump before Colorado's late-summer storm season
Pour water into the pit to confirm it activates, pumps, and shuts off properly
A 15-minute test today. A failed pump during a monsoon storm can flood a basement — $5,000–$30,000 in damage
High
Check fire extinguisher pressure & expiration
Needle should be in the green zone. Replace if expired — an uncharged extinguisher is the same as no extinguisher
Replacement cost: $25–$50. The cost of not having one is incalculable
Med

Heating & Winter Prep3 tasks
Schedule professional furnace inspection
Book in September before HVAC companies fill up — have heat exchanger, burners, and CO output checked
Annual inspection: $80–$150. Emergency furnace failure in a Colorado January runs $300–$600 and risks dangerous CO exposure
High
Replace HVAC filters
Colorado's dry heating season puts extra load on systems — a clean filter is the cheapest maintenance you'll do
A $20 filter protects a $5,000–$12,000 system. Clogged filters are a leading cause of preventable HVAC failure
High
Inspect chimney & fireplace — remove creosote buildup
Have chimney swept and inspected, confirm damper seals properly, and check firebox mortar condition
Chimney cleaning: $150–$250. Creosote-related fires cause catastrophic losses — the prevention cost is trivial by comparison
Med
Plumbing & Freeze Prevention3 tasks
Drain & blow out sprinkler system
This is non-negotiable in Colorado — water left in lines will freeze and crack. Book a professional blowout in September
Professional blowout: $75–$125. Burst sprinkler pipes: $500–$2,500 in repairs. The math is clear
High
Disconnect garden hoses & shut off exterior faucets
A forgotten hose left connected is one of the most common causes of burst pipes — takes 30 seconds
Free to disconnect. A burst pipe from a connected hose causes an average of $5,000+ in water damage
High
Insulate exposed pipes in garage, crawlspace & basement
Pay special attention to pipes on exterior walls and in unheated spaces — Colorado nights get cold fast in fall
Foam pipe insulation: $1–$3 per foot. A burst pipe causes an average of $11,000 in water damage claims
Med
Roof, Gutters & Exterior3 tasks
Clean gutters after leaves fall — do a final pass in November
Clogged gutters in winter trap ice and create ice dams that force water under shingles
Cleaning: $100–$200. Ice dams from blocked gutters cause $4,000–$8,000 in roof and interior damage on average
High
Roof inspection before snow season
Colorado snowpack is heavy and long-lasting — a minor flashing issue becomes a major leak under weeks of snow load
Inspection: $150–$300. Small repair now: $200–$500. Water damage after a Colorado winter: $5,000–$20,000
High
Replace weatherstripping on doors & check garage door seals
Hold a piece of paper in the closed door — if it slides out easily, warm air is escaping all winter
Weatherstripping: $15–$40 per door. Worn seals can add $200–$500 per year in heating costs at Colorado altitudes

Interior Protection3 tasks
Know your water shutoff location — and make sure it works
Every member of your household should be able to shut off the main water valve within 60 seconds
The average water damage claim is $11,000. Knowing your shutoff can dramatically limit damage in those first minutes
High
Monitor indoor humidity levels (30–50% is ideal in Colorado)
Colorado's forced-air heating creates very dry air — too low causes wood floors to crack and shrink; too high causes mold
A $40 humidistat prevents cracked hardwood floors ($2,000–$8,000 to repair) and mold growth ($3,000–$10,000 to remediate)
Med
Check for drafts and cold spots at doors, windows & outlets
Use a stick of incense or your hand — moving air on a still day means you're heating the outside
A drafty Colorado home can add $500–$1,500 to annual heating costs. Sealing pays back within weeks
Med
Snow & Ice3 tasks
Watch for ice dams forming at roof edges
Ice dams form when heat escapes through the roof, melts snow, and refreezes at the cold overhang — act fast when you see them
Ice dam removal: $400–$1,000. Water forced under shingles by ice dams causes $4,000–$8,000 in damage on average
High
Inspect attic for moisture & condensation
Winter condensation in the attic signals inadequate ventilation — find it early before it becomes a mold problem
Attic mold remediation: $3,000–$10,000. Catching condensation in winter costs nothing but a flashlight
Med
Keep walkways clear of snow & ice promptly
Repeated freeze-thaw cycles on untreated concrete accelerate surface spalling — beyond the obvious slip hazard
Concrete resurfacing: $3–$7 per square foot. Prompt snow removal preserves surface integrity through Colorado's long winters
Med
Heating & Safety3 tasks
Test smoke & carbon monoxide detectors
CO risk peaks in winter when heating systems run constantly — CO is odorless and especially dangerous at Colorado's altitude
Working detectors are non-negotiable. This is the one item on this list where skipping it risks lives, not just money
High
Inspect dryer vent for lint buildup
Disconnect and clean the full vent run — not just the trap — at least once a year, more if you do heavy laundry
Dryer fires cause $238M in property damage annually and are almost entirely preventable. 10 minutes, once a year
High
Keep furnace area clear — maintain proper clearance
Nothing flammable within 3 feet of the furnace, and confirm the filter slot is fully sealed when replaced
Heating equipment fires spike every winter and are among the most common causes of house fires — clearance is free

Monthly Recurring Tasks7 tasks
Replace or check HVAC filter
1" filters: monthly. 4" filters: every 3–6 months. When in doubt, hold it to the light
Skipping filters shortens HVAC lifespan by years. System replacement: $5,000–$12,000. The filter: $15–$30
High
Check for plumbing leaks — inspect under all sinks
Look for water stains, soft cabinet floors, and dripping supply lines or drain connections
A slow sink leak costs $10 to fix (a washer). Ignored for months: $1,500–$5,000 in cabinet and subfloor damage
High
Test smoke detectors
Press and hold the test button — it should beep loudly. If it doesn't, replace the battery immediately
30 seconds monthly. Working detectors cut home fire death risk in half — no homeowner should skip this
High
Walk the exterior perimeter after storms
Check foundation, siding, drainage patterns, outdoor fixtures, and any new cracks or settling
A slow foundation crack caught early costs hundreds. Ignored through a season, it costs thousands
Med
Monitor utility bills for unusual spikes
A 20%+ increase in gas or water often signals a hidden leak or HVAC problem before it becomes obvious
Catching an HVAC efficiency problem early: $100–$300 repair. Missing it for a season: $500–$800 in wasted energy
Med
Check gutters after storms
Clear debris that collects after wind or hail events — Colorado's monsoon season is particularly active
Five minutes after a storm. Post-storm gutter clogs overflow and saturate foundation soil — long-term damage, short-term fix
Med
Inspect caulking around tubs, showers & sinks
Look for any separation, cracking, or discoloration — water intrusion behind tile is silent until it's serious
A $6 tube of caulk today. Bathroom mold remediation from ignored grout: $2,000–$8,000
