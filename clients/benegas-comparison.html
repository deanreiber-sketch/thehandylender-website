How these numbers are calculated

Arrive Home Repayable (6.875%)Par rate generates ~$4,934 lender credit on House B (~$4,639 on A). Credit reduces cash to close. With 2/1 BD: 0.25% LLPA (~$1,350–1,436) is offset from the credit first; net credit still reduces CTC. 8.25% repayable 2nd mortgage over 10 years.
Arrive Home Forgivable (7.750%)Par rate — essentially $0 lender credit. 0% 2nd forgiven at 36 months. With 2/1 BD: 0.25% LLPA is a cash cost with no credit to offset it. Both programs follow FHA guidelines for DTI (AUS findings govern).
2/1 Buydown RatesRepayable: Yr1 4.875% → Yr2 5.875% → Yr3+ 6.875%. Forgivable: Yr1 5.750% → Yr2 6.750% → Yr3+ 7.750%. Year 1 and Year 2 payments reflect the reduced rate. Year 3+ is the permanent note rate.
metroDPA (6.750% / EDGE 6.375%)0% interest, 30-yr deferred 2nd — never forgiven, due at sale/refi. Standard 3% or 4% DPA at 6.750%. EDGE 3% at 6.375% with 2% discount point paid from seller concessions. Buydowns not permitted per program guidelines. Max DTI 50%.
