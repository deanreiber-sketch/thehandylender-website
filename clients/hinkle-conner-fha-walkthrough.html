06

Down payment assistance & the 620 line

Where the score really matters
!

The FHA loan can go low — the assistance usually can't

An FHA first mortgage can work with scores down to 580 (or 500 with 10% down). But the down payment assistance that would sit on top of it — a grant or silent second — is a different story: most DPA programs want a 620 minimum, and finding one willing to attach below 620 is genuinely rare.

—

DPA program availability and minimums vary by program and change over time — Dean is confirming current Colorado options. This is for planning, not a commitment of any specific program.
