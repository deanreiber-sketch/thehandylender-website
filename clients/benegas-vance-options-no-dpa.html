How These Numbers Are Calculated

Option 1 — 3/1 Buydown @ 6.875%Note rate 6.875% generates $7,004 lender credit. Combined with $20K seller concessions, $16,034 is available toward the $24,613 3/1 BD subsidy — leaving an $8,579 shortfall the borrower brings. With $7K earnest applied, only $1,579 in additional cash is needed. Payments step up annually: 3.875% → 4.875% → 5.875% → 6.875% from Year 4 onward.
Option 2 — 2/1 Buydown @ 6.375%Rate 6.375% generates a $2,252 lender credit. Combined with $20K concessions, nearly all of the $12,215 2/1 BD subsidy is covered — only $919 shortfall from the borrower. Earnest covers it with $6,081 refund. Payments step: 4.375% → 5.375% → 6.375% permanently from Year 3. Most favorable cash-to-close of the buydown options.
Option 3 — Fixed 5.875%Seller concessions cover all closing costs and fund the $8,932 in points to buy the rate permanently to 5.875%. Gift covers down payment. $0 cash to close — full $7,000 earnest refunded. No buydown period, no payment changes. Highest monthly payment of the three but also the most predictable and the lowest permanent rate.
Gift Fund BreakdownIn all three options, $19,250 of the $20,000 gift goes directly to the down payment. The remaining $750 flows toward closing costs. If the gift is $15,000, a $4,250 down payment gap is created, adding that amount to cash to close in each scenario. Options 2 and 3 at $15K are fully covered by the $7,000 earnest deposit.
Closing Date & Prepaid InterestClosing 6/29/26, first payment 8/1/26 = 2 days prepaid interest. UFMIP ($9,288) is financed into the loan — not in cash to close. All scenarios use standardized HOI 12-month prepaid ($3,360) and 2-month escrow ($560) plus 2-month tax escrow ($617).
Pricing as of 06/02/26Lender credits and points costs based on current FHA 30yr pricing. Option 1 @ 6.875%: $7,004 lender credit. Option 2 @ 6.375%: $2,252 lender credit. Option 3 @ 5.875%: $8,932 points cost. No DPA program in any scenario — clean single FHA loan. Rates subject to change at time of lock. DTI based on $127,742 gross income and $941/mo non-housing debt.
