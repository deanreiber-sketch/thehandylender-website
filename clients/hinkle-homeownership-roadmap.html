Key: Damarko's income alone must still exceed mortgage + debts for DPA eligibility. A LOX confirming co-borrower contribution is required.

Save & Pay Down Debts

Timeline: 18–36 months of focused saving + career advancement could position Damarko for a standalone FHA approval.
