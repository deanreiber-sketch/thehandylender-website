How These Numbers Are Calculated

2/1 Buydown MechanicsSeller funds a buydown escrow at closing. Year 1 rate is 2% below note rate, Year 2 is 1% below, then the permanent note rate applies from Year 3. Scenarios 1 & 2 buydown cost: $13,310.89. Scenario 3: $12,284.87 (lower due to slightly lower rate).
FHA Loan StructureAll scenarios use the same $540,038 loan amount. The $9,288 UFMIP (1.75%) is financed into the loan — not in cash to close. Annual MIP is 0.55% of the base loan ($243/mo). Taxes $308/mo and HOI $280/mo per actual LE figures.
Arrive Home Repayable (Scenarios 1 & 2)3.5% DPA as a 0% interest repayable second mortgage ($19,250 at 8.25% over 10 years = $236/mo). Lender credit at 6.875% par: $5,395 gross less $1,350 LLPA = $4,045 net. Scenario 1 $32K concession covers BD, CC, lender credit offset, and $8,106 points to buy rate to 6.375% — $1,890 remainder reduces DPA 2nd balance.
Scenario 3 — No DPAParents gift the 3.5% down payment ($19,250). With $20K gift: borrower contributes $9,241 and retains ~$10,759 in reserve from own $20K funds. With $30K gift: $0 out of pocket but only ~$759 left in reserve — strongly recommend the $20K option.
Refinance StrategyTarget Year 2 refi when 2/1 buydown expires. Scenarios 1 & 2 must roll $19,250 DPA balance into refi loan ($550,663 combined). Scenario 3 refi loan is just the first mortgage balance ($530,439) — $20K less, lower LTV, and potential path to conventional financing with no MIP.
Pricing as of 05/29/26Scenario 1 & 2: Arrive Home Repayable, FHA 30yr fixed. Scenario 3: FHA Standard Agency 30yr Fixed Buydown @ 6.49% (Mr. Cooper). Rates subject to change at time of lock. DTI calculated on $127,742 gross income and $941/mo non-housing debt.
