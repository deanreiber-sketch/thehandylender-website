Key Notes & Assumptions
⚠ Zillow Points Cost Comparison
  • Zillow charges 3.066 pts ($12,019) + $1,500 origination to achieve 5.99%. Cornerstone achieves the same 5.99% rate at 2.2 pts ($8,624) with no origination fee — saving $4,894 on identical loans.
  • Zillow's $750 appraisal vs Cornerstone's $649 — additional $101 savings.
  • All four scenarios now use identical prepaids, escrow, and deposit structure for a true apples-to-apples comparison.
★ Scenario ④ — The Strongest Overall Structure
  • Lowest Year 1 all-in payment ($2,708/mo) — $276 less than Zillow monthly
  • $20,275 less cash at closing — preserves $17K+ more in liquidity vs Zillow
  • Seller concessions fully fund the 2-1 buydown with $2,696 surplus
  • Zero permanent points = zero break-even risk
  • Unused buydown escrow reduces principal at refinance
  • Year 1 and 2 payments are lower; positioned for free refi when rates fall
† PMI · ‡ Scenario ③ Concession Gap

† PMI at 0.12% annual: $392K × 0.12% ÷ 12 = $39.20/mo. $409K × 0.12% ÷ 12 = $40.90/mo. PMI terminates automatically at 80% LTV.

‡ Scenario ③: Total closing costs + 1-0 buydown ($22,601) exceed the $12,000 seller concession by ~$601. Ryan would cover this small gap, or the owner's title insurance could be deferred to keep costs within concessions.

Aligned Assumptions — All Four Scenarios

28 days prepaid interest  ·  12 months HO insurance premium  ·  $649 appraisal (CFM)  ·  $5,000 earnest deposit  ·  $160/mo HO insurance  ·  $401.80/mo property tax  ·  $35/mo HOA  ·  3 months initial escrow (HO + tax). The only differences between scenarios are loan amount, interest rate, points, and buydown structure.
