The 30-Day Agent Sprint

Ten reasons to reconnect — each one opens a real tool, not a pitch. The goal of every call is a meeting, not a close.

Outreach Call Sheet · 150 Agents · 30 Days
Closing is the midpoint. You re-enter because you’re useful. Calm wins here.
The Ten Hooks

Pick the hook. Open the tool. Ask for 15 minutes.

Warm contacts get the direct asks. Quiet-for-a-year contacts get the send-first plays — you re-enter with a gift, then the call writes itself.

01

The Investor Portfolio System

Asset · Investor Analyzer
Warm ask
Open“Quick question — do you work with real estate investors at all?”
If yes“Then you’ll want to see this. I built a tool that scores six strategies against an investor’s actual numbers and flags when it makes sense to buy or sell. Makes you the sharpest person in their corner. Worth 15 minutes?”
If no“Would you want to? It’s the easiest repeat business an agent never taps. I’ll show you how I’d help you start.”
Show: six-strategy scoring + DSCR vs. conventional
02

“What Kind of Buyer Are You?” Quiz

Asset · Decision Profiles
Send-first
Open“Do you ever get leads who aren’t quite sure what they want yet?”
If yes“I’ve got a 60-second quiz that sorts a buyer into their real profile and hands them a guide built for how they think. Put your name on it, send it to cold leads to warm them up. Happy to show you how agents are using it.”
Show: the six profiles + the co-brandable result page

04

Asset · Ownership Cost Planner

Running the 30 Days

Match the hook to the relationship.

Talked in the last 30 days Warm

Go direct. These contacts already trust the voice, so lead with the meeting asks where you simply open a tool and ask for time.

Use hooks 01 · 04 · 05 · 07 · 08 · 09

Quiet for a year+ Cold

Re-enter with a gift, not a pitch. Send something useful first, then the follow-up call has a reason to exist that isn’t “just checking in.”

Use hooks 02 · 03 · 06 · 10
Cadence: 5–7 touches a day clears all 150 inside the month with room to circle back. Log every reply, and let the second touch be the follow-up — the relationship continues because you’re useful, not because you asked twice.
