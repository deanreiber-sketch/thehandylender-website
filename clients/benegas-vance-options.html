How These Numbers Are Calculated

Option 1 — Arrive Home Repayable + 2/1 BD6.875% generates $5,060 gross lender credit. The 0.25% LLPA for the 2/1 buydown costs $1,350, leaving $3,710 net. BD subsidy $12,515 funded from seller concessions. DPA = $19,250 (3.5% of price) covers full down payment. Repayable 2nd @ 8.25% / 10 yrs = $236/mo. Standard FHA closing cost structure.
Option 2 — metroDPA EDGE (4% DPA)4% DPA of note amount ($21,602) covers the full down payment ($19,250) plus $2,352 toward closing costs. The 2% EDGE discount point ($10,801) is included in origination. Cash closing costs are $31,767 (standardized) due to metroDPA's 1.5% origination fee, Compliance/Admin fee, and Investor Funding Fee. After $20K seller concessions and earnest money, $2,415 still owed at closing. No monthly 2nd payment — deferred balance due at sale or refi.
Option 3 — No DPA / Gift Down PaymentStandard FHA structure, same low closing costs as Option 1 ($11,679). Parents gift $19,250 for the down payment. Seller concessions cover all closing costs ($11,679) leaving $8,321 toward the $14,505 cost to buy the rate to 5.49%. Borrower pays the $6,184 gap — mostly covered by the $7,000 earnest with $816 refund. No second mortgage — ever.
Prepaid InterestClosing 6/29/26 with first payment 8/1/26 means only 2 days of prepaid interest (June 29 and 30). Option 1 @ 6.875%: $203. Option 2 @ 6.25%: $185. Option 3 @ 5.49%: $162. All scenarios standardized with HOI 12-month prepaid ($3,360) and 2-month escrow ($560) at closing.
Option 2 CTCAfter earnest money ($7,000) is applied, $2,415 is still owed at closing. Switching from 3% to 4% DPA saves over $5,000 in CTC by generating $2,352 more in DPA assistance and eliminating the down payment gap. The metroDPA fee structure still results in significantly higher closing costs than the standard FHA options.
