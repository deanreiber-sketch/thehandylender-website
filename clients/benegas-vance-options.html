How These Numbers Are Calculated

Option 1 — Arrive Home Repayable + 2/1 BD6.875% generates $5,060 gross lender credit; $1,350 LLPA for 2/1 BD leaves $3,710 net. BD subsidy $12,515 and closing costs funded from seller concessions. DPA covers full down payment. Repayable 2nd @ 8.25%/10yrs = $236/mo. $395 Arrive Home program fee included.
Option 2 — No DPA, Gift $25K, Perm 5.875% + 2/1 BDGift covers $19,250 down payment + $5,750 toward CC. Seller concessions fund remaining CC ($5,941) + points to 5.875% ($6,356) + 2/1 BD subsidy ($11,900) = $24,197 needed. Shortfall $4,197 covered by earnest — $2,803 refund. No 2nd mortgage. Year 1 effective rate: 3.875%.
Option 3 — No DPA, Gift $25K, 5.375% FixedGift covers $19,250 down + $5,750 toward CC. Seller conc funds remaining CC ($5,926) + $14,074 toward the $16,822 points cost. Shortfall $2,748 from earnest — $4,252 refund. Fixed payment every month, no buydown period, no 2nd mortgage. Lowest permanent rate.
2/1 Buydown MechanicsSeller funds a buydown escrow at closing. Year 1 rate is 2% below note rate, Year 2 is 1% below, Year 3+ is the permanent note rate. Option 1 BD cost: $12,515. Option 2 BD cost: $11,900 (lower because the note rate is lower). Option 3 has no buydown — rate is fixed from day one.
Prepaid InterestClosing 6/29/26, first payment 8/1/26 = 2 days prepaid interest. Option 1 @ 6.875%: $203. Option 2 @ 5.875%: $174. Option 3 @ 5.375%: $159. All scenarios use HOI 12-mo prepaid ($3,360) + 2-mo escrow ($560) and 2-mo tax escrow ($617).
